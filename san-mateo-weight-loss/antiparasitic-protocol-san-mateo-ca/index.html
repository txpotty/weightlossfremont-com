    How the Antiparasitic Protocol Works in San Mateo

    The protocol starts with your symptoms and possible exposures, not with a pill. The provider asks which parasites make sense given where you have traveled, what pets you have and whether people in your household have the same complaints. Testing comes next when it would help. A stool ova and parasite test or a targeted antigen test may be recommended, and the provider explains how to arrange one. Treatment uses prescription anti-parasitics with established uses, and a follow-up confirms symptoms are gone.

    Who This Protocol Is For

    Adults with symptoms or exposures that raise a fair concern for a parasite are the right fit. Nighttime anal itching in a household with kids, which usually means pinworm, is the most common reason people start. Recent travel to regions where intestinal worms are common is another, as is a positive stool test from a previous clinic. It is not a detox. Nor is it a weight loss program.

    Dosing and Schedule in San Mateo

    The schedule depends on the drug and the parasite. For pinworm, a single dose of mebendazole 100 mg or albendazole 400 mg is typical, with a second dose two weeks later for newly hatched worms. Ivermectin is dosed by weight. It is usually given as one dose or a short course of one to two days. Your provider writes out exact dates for every dose.

    What to Expect Week by Week

    Most courses take one to three days, sometimes with a repeat dose two weeks later. A day or two of mild stomach upset or tiredness after dosing is common. Pinworm itching often improves within several days, though it can take a week or more to fade completely as the last eggs clear. Week two is the repeat dose. A check or a follow-up test may be suggested about a month after treatment.

    Side Effects and Precautions in San Mateo

    Nausea, abdominal pain, diarrhea and headache are the side effects people mention most often. With ivermectin, a temporary rash or itch sometimes appears as the parasites die. Mebendazole and albendazole are poorly absorbed into the blood when used for intestinal worms, which keeps most side effects mild at short courses. Pregnancy changes things. Several of these drugs are not used during pregnancy, and the provider will ask.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks when symptoms started, where you have traveled in the past year and whether pets or family members are involved. Prior test results help a lot. Providers also screen for pregnancy, breastfeeding, liver disease and current medications that could interact, such as warfarin with ivermectin. Bloody stools, high fever or severe belly pain lead to an in-person referral. Some people are asked to finish testing before any medication is prescribed.

    How the Protocol Compares to Over-the-Counter Cleanses in San Mateo

    Herbal parasite cleanses sold online are not regulated as medications. Their ingredients and doses vary widely from bottle to bottle, and most have never been tested against a specific infection in people. Prescription drugs come with set dosing. Ivermectin and mebendazole, both offered individually through Fremont Weight Loss, each work best against particular parasites. Your provider picks one drug rather than layering several products.
