    How Zepbound Treatment Works in Union City, CA

    How Zepbound Works in Union City

    Tirzepatide acts on two gut hormone receptors. GIP and GLP-1 together help regulate appetite, slow how fast the stomach empties and improve the way the body handles blood sugar after a meal. Most people feel full on smaller portions. You inject it once weekly into the belly, thigh or upper arm.

    Who Zepbound Is For

    Zepbound is approved for adults with a BMI of 30 or higher. Adults with a BMI of 27 or more also qualify if they have a condition tied to weight, such as high blood pressure, type 2 diabetes, high cholesterol or sleep apnea. In late 2024 the FDA added moderate to severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity. It is not approved for children.

    Zepbound Dosing Schedule in Union City

    Everyone starts at 2.5 mg. That first month is about letting your stomach adjust, not about weight loss, so modest early changes are normal. From there, doses climb in 2.5 mg steps every four weeks or more, through 5, 7.5, 10 and 12.5 mg to a top of 15 mg. Most people settle at 5 mg, 10 mg or 15 mg.

    What to Expect Week by Week in Union City

    The first month is mostly quiet. You may notice a smaller appetite within the first week, and stomach upset tends to cluster in the day or two after a shot or a dose increase. The SURMOUNT-1 clinical study reported average weight loss of roughly 20 percent at the 15 mg dose over 72 weeks. Individual results vary.

    Zepbound Side Effects

    Nausea and diarrhea come up most. Vomiting, constipation, indigestion, stomach pain and redness at the injection site are also common, especially during the first days after each dose increase. Rarer and more serious problems include pancreatitis, gallstones, dehydration that harms the kidneys and allergic reactions. Zepbound carries a boxed warning about thyroid C-cell tumors seen in rodents.

    Eligibility and Screening in Union City

    Your provider starts with height, weight and BMI. Next comes your medical history and every medication you take, with special attention to insulin, sulfonylureas and birth control pills. People with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 cannot take Zepbound, and it is not used during pregnancy. Labs may be requested.

    Zepbound Compared With Related Options in Union City

    Zepbound and Mounjaro are the same medicine. The only real difference is the approved use, weight for Zepbound and type 2 diabetes for Mounjaro, which affects coverage and paperwork. A direct comparison trial found larger average weight loss with tirzepatide than with Wegovy. Compounded tirzepatide comes from compounding pharmacies and is not FDA-approved.
