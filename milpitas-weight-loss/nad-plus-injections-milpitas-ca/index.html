    How NAD+ Injection Therapy Works in Milpitas, CA

    How NAD+ Works in Milpitas

    NAD+ is nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide. In every cell it carries electrons through the reactions that turn food into ATP, and it is consumed by sirtuins and PARPs, enzymes tied to DNA repair and stress responses. Levels decline with age. A subcutaneous injection places NAD+ under the skin, where it is absorbed into the bloodstream without passing through the digestive tract, though how much reaches each tissue is still being studied.

    Who NAD+ Injections Are Usually For

    Adults interested in energy, mental clarity, exercise recovery and healthy aging are the usual candidates. Some add NAD+ during GLP-1 weight loss to support how they feel while eating less. NAD+ does not treat disease or cause weight loss, and pregnancy, breastfeeding, active cancer or serious liver or kidney disease usually rule it out.

    NAD+ Dosing and Schedule in Milpitas

    Small shots, slow push. A common start is roughly 50 milligrams under the skin a few times weekly, with increases every week or two depending on how you feel. Belly and thigh are the usual sites. A slow injection makes a real difference.

    What to Expect in the First Month

    Week one is about technique. Many people feel warmth, flushing, mild nausea or a tight feeling right after injecting, and it usually fades within minutes. Over weeks two to four, some patients report steadier energy or clearer thinking, while others notice little change. Your provider adjusts the ramp at check-ins.

    NAD+ Injection Side Effects in Milpitas

    Flushing leads the list. Other frequent ones are stinging at the site, nausea, headache and feeling lightheaded. Chest tightness, muscle cramps or a racing heart can happen if the dose is too high or pushed too fast, and these usually improve with a lower dose or slower injection. Rare allergic reactions need emergency care.

    Eligibility and Screening

    The review starts with history and your supplement list. Expect questions on cancer, pregnancy, organ health, heart rhythm and whether you use NMN, NR or high-dose niacin. Labs are not always required. Upload recent labs if you have them.

    NAD+ Injections Compared to Other Options in Milpitas

    IV infusions are the usual comparison. IV NAD+ puts more in at once, though sessions often last hours and the discomfort during the drip is a common complaint. NAD+ nasal spray avoids needles at a smaller dose. Oral precursors like NMN and nicotinamide riboside are supplements that help the body make its own NAD+.
