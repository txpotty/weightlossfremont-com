    How Dutasteride + Minoxidil Works in Pleasanton

    Who This Combination Is For

    It is mainly for adult men with male pattern hair loss who did not get enough from finasteride.     Dosing and Schedule in Pleasanton

    Dutasteride is FDA-approved at 0.5 mg daily for an enlarged prostate and is used off-label at that dose for hair loss. Its long half-life lets some providers use fewer doses per week. Minoxidil is applied daily or taken orally. Low-dose oral minoxidil is an off-label alternative.

    Shedding can pick up in months one and two, usually from minoxidil. That early shed is common, and it typically settles by month three as follicles move into a new growth phase. By months three to six, many men notice less loss and some thickening. Comparison trials favored dutasteride over finasteride for hair counts. Give it a year.
