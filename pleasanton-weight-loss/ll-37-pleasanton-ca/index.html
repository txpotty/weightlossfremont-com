    How LL-37 Works in Pleasanton

    LL-37 is cut from a larger protein called hCAP18, and it is the only cathelicidin the human body makes, named for the 2 leucines at its start and its 37 amino acids. In lab studies it punches holes in bacterial and fungal membranes. It also signals other immune cells and has been studied for wound healing. Mostly preclinical. Early trials looked at topical LL-37 on chronic leg ulcers, but no LL-37 product is FDA-approved.

    Who Usually Asks About LL-37
