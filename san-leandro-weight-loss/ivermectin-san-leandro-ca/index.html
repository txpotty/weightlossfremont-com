    How Ivermectin Works in San Leandro

    Ivermectin binds to channels in the nerve and muscle cells of certain parasites. That causes paralysis and death of the organism. Human nerve cells are far less sensitive at prescribed doses, mainly because the drug is kept out of the brain by a barrier that parasites do not have. The tablets carry FDA approval for strongyloides infection of the intestine and for river blindness. Clinicians also use it for scabies and lice in appropriate cases.

    Who Ivermectin Is For

    Adults with a confirmed or strongly suspected strongyloides infection are the classic candidates. This infection is often picked up through bare skin contact with soil in warm climates, and it can stay in the body for decades. It is also used for scabies. Crusted scabies, outbreaks in a household or cases where permethrin cream has failed are where oral ivermectin helps most. It has no role in weight loss.

    Ivermectin Dosing and Schedule in San Leandro

    Ivermectin is dosed by body weight, commonly around 200 micrograms per kilogram as a single oral dose. For a 180 pound adult, that usually works out to about five or six 3 mg tablets, rounded by the provider. Take it on an empty stomach with water. Strongyloides is often treated with one or two daily doses, and scabies usually gets two doses about 7 to 14 days apart. Your label lists the exact tablet count and dates.

    What to Expect After Treatment

    Active treatment is one or two doses, so the hard part is usually waiting. A day of mild dizziness or nausea is not unusual. Scabies itch can last two to four weeks after the mites are gone, because the skin keeps reacting while it heals, and that alone is not treatment failure. Hot-wash your bedding. Expect a check-in around week four.

    Ivermectin Side Effects in San Leandro

    Dizziness, nausea, diarrhea and itching are the common side effects. As parasites die, some people get a short-lived rash or low fever. Serious effects are uncommon. Confusion, unsteady walking and severe rashes are rare at correct doses, and most cases reported to poison centers involve animal products or self-dosing. Confusion or loss of balance means urgent care, the same day.

    Eligibility and Screening

    Your intake asks about symptoms, travel history, body weight, pregnancy and breastfeeding. Medications come next. Warfarin is a known interaction, and some drugs that affect brain chemistry or liver enzymes need a closer look before ivermectin is prescribed. People with serious liver disease or some neurological disorders may need another option. Suspected strongyloides often needs an antibody test or stool test first.

    How Ivermectin Compares to Other Anti-Parasitics in San Leandro

    Mebendazole and albendazole are usually preferred for pinworm, roundworm, whipworm and hookworm. Ivermectin is the first choice for strongyloides. For scabies, topical permethrin 5 percent cream is still a standard first step in many cases, with oral ivermectin used for crusted scabies, household outbreaks or permethrin failures. The antiparasitic protocol pulls these together when the parasite is not yet clear. Your provider picks the drug with the best evidence.
